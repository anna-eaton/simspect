B. Channels Protected
InvisiSpec blocks covert and side channels that are constructed by monitoring the state of any level of a multiprocessor
data cache hierarchy. This includes cache state such as
occupancy (e.g., what lines live in what sets), replacement
information, and coherence information (e.g., whether a line
is in the Exclusive or Shared state). It also includes data TLB
state (e.g., what entries live in the TLB). Our goal is to provide
a complete defense for the data cache hierarchy,2 as it has
been proven to lead to practical attacks [1], [2]. Note that our
mechanism does not preclude protecting additional channels.
