\textbf{The visibility-point/squash race in STT, on a real gem5 trace} (STT build with commitToIEWDelay $=4$, test inst-047231; flag-setting instructions omitted from the listing). The transmitter i5 is ready to issue at cycle 49 but is held because its address comes from the speculative load i3. At cycle 77 the mispredicted branch i2 resolves; STT immediately stops treating i3's result as tainted, and i5 issues at 78 and sends a cache packet at 79 ($\star$). The squash caused by the same resolution reaches the load queue only at 81. Cycle 0 is the retirement of the last committed instruction; the committed window closes when fnc retires at 261. At stock timing (commitToIEWDelay $=1$) the squash wins and no packet is sent.
